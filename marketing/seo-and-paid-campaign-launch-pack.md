# Orynex Studio — SEO & Paid Campaign Launch Pack

Status: draft assets prepared; no advertising accounts connected and no paid campaigns launched.

## Brand positioning

**Core message:** Human-first digital products, thoughtfully designed and engineered to perform.

**Services to promote**
- Digital Product Design (UI/UX and responsive product experiences)
- Technology Architecture (cloud and systems architecture)
- Custom Engineering (web applications, integrations, and APIs)
- Performance & Optimization (website speed and Core Web Vitals)

**Primary conversion:** qualified project enquiry through the website contact form.

## SEO baseline implemented

- Descriptive page title and meta description
- Canonical URL for the currently deployed Vercel site
- Open Graph and X/Twitter sharing metadata
- Organization JSON-LD structured data
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`

Before launch, verify the canonical domain choice. If `orynex.studio` becomes the working, verified domain, update the canonical URL, structured data, Open Graph URL, sitemap and robots sitemap together. Do not submit both domains as canonical versions.

## Measurement checklist before spending

- [ ] Confirm the primary domain and redirect/canonical behavior.
- [ ] Connect Google Search Console and submit the sitemap.
- [ ] Connect GA4 and configure a consent/privacy approach appropriate to the audience and jurisdictions.
- [ ] Track successful contact-form submissions as a lead conversion (not just button clicks).
- [ ] If importing Google Ads conversions, configure the Google tag or supported GA4 import and verify it in diagnostics.
- [ ] Add UTM parameters to social campaign links.
- [ ] Test the form on mobile and confirm enquiries reach the intended team.
- [ ] Confirm target locations, language, monthly/daily budget, offer, and who will respond to leads.
- [ ] Check Google Ads and Meta account access, billing, ad policies, and final URLs before publishing.

## Required owner decisions

1. Target country/cities and service-language(s).
2. Daily or monthly ad spend ceiling.
3. Preferred lead type: design projects, custom software, architecture, or all services.
4. Google Ads customer account and GA4 property access (do not share passwords or secret keys in chat).
5. Meta Business/Instagram and YouTube channel access for publishing.
6. Whether the verified custom domain will be used instead of the Vercel URL.
