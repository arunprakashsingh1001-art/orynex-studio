# Orynex Studio — Zero-Budget Organic Growth Pack

Status: organic-first plan for a worldwide audience. Advertising budget: 0. No paid campaigns are planned or launched.

## Brand positioning

**Core message:** Human-first digital products, thoughtfully designed and engineered to perform.

**Services to promote**
- Digital Product Design (UI/UX and responsive product experiences)
- Technology Architecture (cloud and systems architecture)
- Custom Engineering (web applications, integrations, and APIs)
- Performance & Optimization (website speed and Core Web Vitals)

**Primary conversion:** qualified project enquiry through the website contact form.

## SEO baseline prepared

- Descriptive page title and meta description
- Canonical URL for the currently deployed Vercel site
- Open Graph and X/Twitter sharing metadata
- Organization JSON-LD structured data
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`

### Free SEO launch checklist

- [ ] Merge and deploy the SEO changes.
- [ ] Open Google Search Console and add the deployed website as a property.
- [ ] Verify site ownership using a supported method.
- [ ] Submit `https://orynexstudio.vercel.app/sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Inspect the homepage URL and check indexing status.
- [ ] Review search queries and impressions after Google has had time to crawl.
- [ ] Keep one preferred canonical domain. If `orynex.studio` becomes verified and ready, update the canonical URL, structured data, social metadata, sitemap and robots sitemap together.
- [ ] Create genuinely useful, original pages for each core service, with clear examples, process, FAQs and contact CTA. Avoid thin pages created only to repeat keywords.

## Worldwide organic positioning

No country or region is being prioritized. Use clear international English and avoid claiming local presence, local offices, region-specific expertise, or service guarantees unless those facts are verified. The website should explain whether remote projects are welcome, typical collaboration hours, and which languages the team can support—after the owner confirms these details.

## Instagram — organic Reels and carousels

**Posting cadence:** start with 3 short Reels and 1 carousel per week, at a pace the team can sustain. Reuse each idea as a YouTube Short where appropriate.

### Reel concept A: From rough idea to clear product

- 0–3s: Rough wireframe. Text: “A good idea deserves a better digital experience.”
- 3–7s: Transition into a polished responsive interface. Text: “Human-first product design.”
- 7–12s: Show mobile/desktop states and interaction details. Text: “Thoughtful UI. Clear user journeys.”
- 12–16s: Brand end card. Text: “Design. Engineering. Performance.”
- CTA in caption: “Planning a digital product? Explore Orynex Studio and tell us what you’re building.”

### Reel concept B: Three website details that build trust

- Show mobile responsiveness, clear hierarchy, and an obvious contact path.
- Explain each in one short sentence.
- End with: “Small details make a better experience.”

### Carousel concepts

1. “5 signs your website needs a UX refresh”
2. “What happens between an idea and a working web app?”
3. “UI/UX design vs. development: how they work together”
4. “A practical website performance checklist”

**Caption template:**  
Building or improving a digital product? Orynex Studio works across product design, custom engineering, technology architecture, and performance optimization. Explore our work and share your project goals through the website.

**Organic distribution:** add accurate captions and alt text, use a small set of specific topic hashtags, engage meaningfully with relevant product/design/engineering discussions, and share helpful answers rather than spam or mass unsolicited DMs.

## YouTube — organic Shorts and tutorials

### Short A (15 seconds)

- 0–3s: “Have a digital product idea?”
- 3–8s: Fast cuts: user flow → interface → responsive implementation.
- 8–12s: “Design. Engineering. Performance.”
- 12–15s: “Build with purpose.” Show Orynex Studio branding and website URL.

### Short B (20 seconds)

- Hook: “A beautiful website is only the beginning.”
- Show three steps: understand the user, design the flow, build and test responsively.
- End: “Follow for practical product design and engineering insights.”

### Longer video ideas

- “How to plan a website before writing code”
- “What makes a UI easier to use?”
- “A beginner-friendly Core Web Vitals checklist”
- “From requirements to a responsive prototype”
- “How to scope a custom web application”

Use accurate titles, helpful descriptions, subtitles, consistent thumbnails, and a clear website link. Do not use misleading clickbait or unsupported performance/client-result claims.

## Free distribution and credibility

- Complete relevant free social profiles and link to the official website.
- Keep the brand name, service descriptions, logo, and website URL consistent.
- Share original mini case studies only when the project and permission are real.
- Publish useful service pages and educational articles that answer real customer questions.
- Participate in relevant professional communities with genuinely helpful answers and follow each community's rules.
- Ask satisfied clients for honest testimonials only when applicable; never invent clients, reviews, awards, partner logos, or results.
- Make sure every enquiry receives a timely, professional response.

## Paid advertising status

Google Ads, Meta/Instagram ads, and YouTube paid placements are **out of scope while the budget is zero**. No campaigns should be enabled, and no billing setup is needed for the organic plan. Existing creative ideas may be adapted into organic posts without purchasing reach.
