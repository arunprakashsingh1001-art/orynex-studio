## Carousel template (6 slides)

1. Hook: “A better [website/product] starts with [principle].”
2. Problem: explain a common friction point without shaming the audience.
3. Principle: one useful rule.
4. Example: real authorized work or clearly labelled concept.
5. Checklist: 2–3 actionable takeaways.
6. Brand end card: “Your Idea Deserves a Better Digital Experience.” / “Design. Engineering. Performance.” / “Explore Our Work.”

## Comment reply templates

**Positive comment:**  
“Thanks for watching! Which part of the design or build process would you like us to break down next?”

**Question about services:**  
“Thanks for your interest in Orynex Studio. You can explore our work at https://orynexstudio.vercel.app/. If you share a little about what you're building, we can point you toward the most relevant service.”

**Request for price:**  
“Pricing depends on the scope, requirements, and timeline. Share a short project overview through our website and we can understand what you need before discussing next steps.”

**Critical feedback:**  
“Thanks for sharing that perspective. We’ll review it. Was there a specific part you felt could be clearer or more useful?”

**Spam or abuse:**  
Do not engage. Use platform moderation/reporting tools where appropriate.

## Enquiry reply template

Subject/message: Thanks for reaching out to Orynex Studio

“Thanks for contacting Orynex Studio. To understand your project, could you share:
1. What are you hoping to build or improve?
2. Who will use it?
3. What is the most important outcome?
4. Is there a target timeline or budget range you would like us to consider?
5. Are there any examples or links we should review?

Please do not send passwords, payment credentials, or other sensitive information. Once we understand the scope, we can advise on a sensible next step.”

## Hashtag rule

Use a small set of relevant, accurate hashtags; tailor to the specific video. Avoid unrelated trending tags and repetitive tag stuffing. Example pool: #OrynexStudio #WebDevelopment #DigitalProductDesign #SoftwareEngineering #UXDesign #DigitalExperience.
