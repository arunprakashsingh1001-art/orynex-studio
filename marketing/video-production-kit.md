# Video Production Kit — Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts

## Master specifications

- Canvas: vertical 9:16; target export 1080×1920.
- Duration: usually 15–25 seconds for these scripts; test rather than treating this as a universal rule.
- Visual identity: matte black/charcoal, vivid orange accent, restrained glow, clean typography, cinematic contrast.
- Use readable text, strong contrast, captions, and safe margins. Preview inside each platform before publishing.
- Audio: original or properly licensed; verify commercial-use rights.
- End card: Orynex Studio; “Your Idea Deserves a Better Digital Experience.”; “Design. Engineering. Performance.”; CTA “Explore Our Work”.
- Destination: https://orynexstudio.vercel.app/

## Script 01 — Brand film (20 seconds)

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voice-over / sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Sketch or abstract idea on a dark desk; orange light enters frame | Every great digital experience starts with an idea. | Soft impact, restrained build |
| 2–6s | Wireframes and design system elements assemble | Thoughtful design | “Start with a clear idea.” |
| 6–10s | Code, architecture, interface components | Purposeful engineering | “Bring it to life with design and engineering.” |
| 10–14s | Responsive desktop/mobile concept screens | Built around the experience | “Shape the details around the people who use it.” |
| 14–17s | Product visual resolves into a clean composition | Design. Engineering. Performance. | Music resolves |
| 17–20s | Brand end card and website CTA | Orynex Studio / Explore Our Work | “Your idea deserves a better digital experience.” |

Full VO: “Every great digital experience starts with an idea. Thoughtful design. Purposeful engineering. A digital experience shaped around your goals. Orynex Studio. Your idea deserves a better digital experience. Explore our work.”

## Script 02 — Website concept transformation (15 seconds)

- 0–3s: Rough wireframe. Text: “What could this idea become?”
- 3–6s: Layout, typography, and hierarchy evolve. Text: “Start with clarity.”
- 6–10s: Show responsive desktop and mobile concept. Text: “Design for real screens.”
- 10–12s: Show navigation and interaction states. Text: “Make every detail count.”
- 12–15s: Brand end card. Text: “Orynex Studio — Explore Our Work.”

Caption: “A concept-to-interface exploration. Good digital experiences are shaped through thoughtful decisions, iteration, and attention to detail. Explore our work: https://orynexstudio.vercel.app/”

Label speculative work “concept” or “demo”; never present it as a client result.

## Script 03 — Three services (20 seconds)

- 0–2s hook: “One idea. Several important decisions.”
- 2–7s digital product design: user flow, wireframe, UI. Overlay: “Design the experience.”
- 7–12s custom engineering: code/editor and component view. Overlay: “Build with purpose.”
- 12–16s architecture and optimization: simplified diagram and performance review. Overlay: “Think beyond launch.”
- 16–20s end card: “Design. Engineering. Performance.” / Orynex Studio / “Explore Our Work.”

## Script 04 — Practical website tip (20 seconds)

- 0–2s hook: “One homepage improvement to try today.”
- 2–7s show a cluttered *illustrative concept* and highlight competing elements.
- 7–13s show one clear headline, one supporting sentence, one primary CTA.
- 13–17s explain: “Make the next step obvious.”
- 17–20s end card and CTA.

Avoid implying this single change guarantees conversions.

## Script 05 — Engineering explainer (20 seconds)

- 0–3s: “A good interface is only part of the experience.”
- 3–8s: show an illustrative component flow.
- 8–14s: show states, error handling, and responsive layout.
- 14–17s: “Reliable details matter.”
- 17–20s: Orynex Studio end card.

## Script 06 — Performance mindset (15 seconds)

- 0–3s: “Performance is part of the experience.”
- 3–7s: show a page loading sequence or a labelled demo.
- 7–11s: show image optimization, reduced clutter, or responsive checks.
- 11–15s: “Design. Engineering. Performance.” / Orynex Studio / “Explore Our Work.”

Only state specific measured speed gains if they have actually been measured under a documented test setup.

## Editing checklist

- [ ] Hook lands immediately.
- [ ] Every shot adds information; remove filler.
- [ ] Transitions support the message instead of distracting.
- [ ] Captions match the voice-over and remain legible.
- [ ] Orange is an accent, not a wall of glow.
- [ ] Logo/brand mark is an approved asset; if not available, use clean text branding.
- [ ] No unlicensed music, stock, fonts, or imagery.
- [ ] No credentials, private client information, or personal data on screen.
- [ ] End card is visible long enough to read.
- [ ] Export reviewed with sound and muted.
- [ ] Create a clean master and a platform-specific version.
- [ ] Save source project, final export, cover, captions, and rights notes.
